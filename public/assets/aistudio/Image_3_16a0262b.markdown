## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a welcoming, patient, and ultra-accessible digital environment tailored to elderly Slovak citizens navigating digital tools and public e-services. The emotional tone avoids cold institutional rigidity, conveying reassurance, dignity, and calm competence. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism with High-Contrast Functionalism:
- High typographic legibility prioritizing cognitive ease and spatial orientation.
- Generous, uncluttered compositions that minimize visual noise and decision paralysis.
- Obvious affordances with explicit boundaries, prominent interactive states, and physical clarity over decorative abstractions.

## Colors
The color palette is calibrated for AAA WCAG compliance across all text tiers against warm, soft light canvases.

- **Primary (`#1E40AF`)**: Deep royal blue communicating reliability, authority, and safety. Used for primary calls-to-action, active navigation states, and key headers.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`)**: Balanced therapeutic teal used for reassuring cues, verification stamps, helpful tips, and auxiliary action items.
- **Neutral Deep (`#0F172A`)**: Near-black slate for all critical reading typography and crisp border lines, avoiding harsh pure black while maintaining maximum contrast (ratio > 12:1 against background).
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft, warm off-white canvas preventing ocular fatigue caused by stark white screens.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white elevated surface for cards and interactive inputs to establish distinct spatial layering.
- **Border Subtle (`#CBD5E1`)**: High-visibility boundary line (minimum 1.5px to 2px width) ensuring visible element edges for low-vision users.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans was chosen for its open counters, distinct letterforms (such as disambiguated 'I', 'l', and '1'), and humanist curves, which reduce reading fatigue among elderly readers.

- The baseline standard for reading content is `body-lg` (20px) with generous line heights (`1.6x` to `1.65x`) to maintain focus across long sentences.
- No interactive or informative text drops below 16px.
- When the global font size switcher (`A- / A+`) is activated, typography scales proportionally across rem roots (100% -> 115% -> 130%), with containers adapting layout flow without text truncation or clipping.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs an 8-column (tablet) to 12-column (desktop) fluid grid constrained within a maximum readable content width of 1140px. On mobile, a single-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters ensures elements span the full readable width without cramped margins.

- Spacing between consecutive interactive elements must never fall below 16px to prevent accidental taps.
- Vertical flow prioritizes generous paragraph separation (`space-md` to `space-lg`), giving older users breathing room to process information step-by-step.
- Touch and click zones enforce an absolute physical minimum hit area of 52px × 52px across all form factors.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through defined borders combined with soft ambient light rather than deep drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background surface tint `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`) and an ambient shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Focus)**: Surface border intensifies to 2px `#1E40AF` with a subtle elevation lift `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Accessibility Bar)**: Pure white background, 2px border `#94A3B8`, and diffused shadow `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Focus Rings**: Universal 3px solid focus ring in `#2563EB` offset by 2px from element borders for total keyboard navigation clarity.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of 2 establishes smooth, tactile corners without overly circular or playful forms that could degrade clarity or reduce clickable surface area.

- Standard buttons, form fields, and dropdowns utilize 8px (`0.5rem`) corner rounding.
- Content cards, step-by-step guides, and callout boxes utilize 16px (`1rem`) corner rounding.
- Pill badges and the top accessibility toggle bar use 24px (`1.5rem`) to stand out from primary content blocks.

## Components

### Accessibility Action Bar (A- / A+ / Audio Read-Out)
- Fixed at the top-right of the header or sticky toolbar.
- High-contrast segmented control: 54px button heights with explicit textual labels ("Písmo: Štandardné / Väčšie" or "A- / A+ / A++") accompanied by a speaker icon for text-to-speech.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#1E40AF` fill with crisp white text (`label-lg`), minimum height of 54px, horizontal padding `space-lg` (32px), with clear 2px border in `#1E3A8A`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background with 2px solid `#1E40AF` border and `#1E40AF` text. Hover shifts background to `#EFF6FF`.
- **Feedback States**: Visible visual transformation on click/tap, providing haptic-like press animation (1px downward shift) and explicit high-contrast hover feedback.

### Breadcrumbs
- Prominent breadcrumb trails placed directly beneath the global navigation.
- Minimum font size 18px with large arrow separators (`→` or heavy chevron icons) and underlines on parent links to guarantee recognizability.

### Cards (Q&A Topics & Guides)
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 2px structural border `#E2E8F0` and 24px inner padding.
- Card titles utilize `headline-sm` with an explicit colored category badge (e.g., "Internet", "Bezpečnosť", "Smartfón").
- Includes an explicit "Čítať odpoveď" (Read Answer) action link with an icon, ensuring users know the entire card is actionable.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- Massive 56px minimum height with large placeholder text (18px) in `#64748B`.
- 2px border in `#94A3B8` by default, expanding to 3px `#1E40AF` when focused.
- Clear search and submit buttons embedded with explicit Slovak text labels rather than standalone icons.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Enlarged hit boxes (28px × 28px visual indicator inside a 52px target zone).
- High-contrast checked state with bold ticks or inner discs (minimum 4px stroke) ensuring visibility without squinting.

### Step-by-Step Instructions (Accordion / Linear Flow)
- Numbered circular badges (36px × 36px) with bold numerals.
- Large expandable trigger areas spanning full width with explicit "Rozbaliť / Zabaliť" (Expand / Collapse) indicator badges.
